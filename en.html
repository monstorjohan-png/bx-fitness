About

Where Movement Meets Style — a gym & spa in New Cairo

On Mivida Street in the Fifth Settlement of New Cairo, BX Fitness Hub brings the gym and the spa under one roof: a modern weight-training floor, group classes, HYROX prep, a coaching team, and the BX Spa for Moroccan bath, massage and relaxation.

  • Gym & weights — a full training floor with free weights and machines
  • Classes & HYROX — group training and functional performance
  • Coaches — a training team that guides you step by step
  • BX Spa — Moroccan bath · massage · spa & wellness
